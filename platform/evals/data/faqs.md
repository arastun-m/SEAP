# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) in Smart Campus Energy Management

Questions include:  
- System usage and automation  
- Feedback and learning processes  
- Data refresh and access  
- Alerts, overrides, and protocols  
- Sustainability initiatives

---
**Q1:** *Why did my HVAC turn off automatically?*  
**A1:** The system uses occupancy sensors and scheduling to detect when spaces are unused, then applies automated energy-saving routines.

**Q2:** *How often is data updated?*  
**A2:** Sensor data is refreshed approximately every 5 minutes. Visualization dashboards show near real-time data across supported buildings and rooms.

**Q3:** *Who can access my energy usage data?*  
**A3:** Your data is private. Only you and authorized campus administrators (with proper access roles) can view individual-level usage data. Privacy is governed under campus data governance policies.

**Q4:** *Can I get alerts for excessive energy usage?*  
**A4:** Yes. You can enable alerts in your user profile to notify you when energy use exceeds a custom or campus-defined threshold.

**Q5:** *Where do I report incorrect data or dashboard errors?*  
**A5:** Use the in-app feedback form or chatbot to report anomalies. You can also contact your local Sustainability Champion or the campus Energy Team.

**Q6:** *How does the system learn from feedback?*  
**A6:** Feedback is reviewed weekly by system admins. Relevant suggestions may influence adjustments in automation rules, HVAC tuning, or sustainability policies.

**Q7:** *Does the system work during emergencies or outages?*  
**A7:** Yes, critical systems remain active via local backup services. In the event of fire drills or power cuts, automations pause, and emergency override protocols take effect.

**Q8:** *Can I access the system outside campus?*  
**A8:** Yes. All web and mobile tools are securely accessible off-campus with multi-factor authentication (MFA) for authorized users.

**Q9:** *What sustainability policies does the system support?*  
**A9:** The platform aligns with Sustainable Procurement, Business Travel, Green Labs, Waste Minimization, and Biodiversity Action Plan initiatives. Admin dashboards can reflect related metrics and sustainability scores.

**Q10:** *Are lab users supported in sustainability efforts?*  
**A10:** Yes. The system highlights lab energy usage and shares LEAF performance badges for participating groups. You can also apply for the Sustainable Lab Kit Fund for equipment upgrades.

**Q11:** *Where can I find sustainability guides or policies?*  
**A11:** Use the chatbot or visit the ‘Sustainability Protocols’ section in your dashboard to access documents such as the Net Zero Pathway, Travel Policy, Procurement Strategy, and the Biodiversity Plan.

**Q12:** *How does the system align with university-wide Net Zero goals?*  
**A12:** The system contributes to real-time monitoring, usage transparency, and policy compliance tracking—essential components of the Net Zero 2040 strategy. Key building efficiency KPIs and emissions dashboards are available to admins.
