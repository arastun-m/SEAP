# Sustainability Initiatives in the University Campus

Our University is committed to becoming a global leader in sustainability, with a strategic goal to achieve **net zero carbon emissions across its estate by 2040**. The initiatives below reflect University’s multi-dimensional approach to climate action, environmental stewardship, and sustainable operations.

---

## 1. Net Zero Estate by 2040

- Committed to achieving **net zero scope 1 and 2 emissions without offsetting** by 2040.
- Published a roadmap outlining decarbonisation across 100+ buildings on 11 campuses.
- Initiatives include:
  - Replacement of steam networks with **low-temperature hot water systems**
  - Installation of **LED lighting**, solar panels, and advanced building controls
  - Integration of **Building Management Systems (BMS)** with smart sensors

---

## 2. Sustainable Procurement Policy

- **New procurement policy and toolkit** launched in 2024.
- Sustainability weightings:
  - **20%** in major contracts
  - **10%** in all other contracts
- Commitments:
  - Assess all suppliers on environmental policies by 2026
  - Create a **Carbon Emissions Analysis Tool** to map procurement emissions by department

---

## 3. Sustainable Business Travel

- Policy requires **climate-conscious travel decisions** and prioritizes low-carbon transport.
- Targets:
  - 25% reduction in carbon emissions per staff FTE from air travel by 2025–26 (vs 2017–18)
- Measures:
  - Flight reduction targets, greener travel options, and offsetting when required by funders

---

## 4. Green Labs Programme

- Participation in the **LEAF framework** (30% of labs accredited in 2023–24).
  - 298 labs awarded Bronze, 45 Silver, 19 Gold
- **My Green Lab Freezer Challenge** saved 283,842 kWh and 58 tCO₂
- Introduced the **Sustainable Lab Kit Fund** (£50,000+) to subsidize eco-friendly equipment

---

## 5. Biodiversity and Green Spaces

- Launched a **Biodiversity Working Group** and plan to appoint a permanent Head Gardener.
- Key goals:
  - Improve biodiversity on green roofs, walls, and gardens
  - Implement a verified **Biodiversity Action Plan** by 2025–26
- Example project: Restoration of the ornamental pond at Silwood Park Campus

---

## 6. Energy and ICT Optimisation

- De-steaming project resulted in:
  - 2,400 tonnes CO₂ reduction per annum
  - Increased heating efficiency to 87%
- ICT-led **Carbon Emissions Analysis Tool** enables departments to track their emissions
- Pilot programs:
  - Smart energy meters
  - BMS integration with standalone A/C units

---

## 7. Low-Carbon Catering

- Removed **86% of beef** from menus (full removal planned by Oct 2024)
- New low-energy dishwashing machines and **rainforest-certified sourcing**
- Event catering to become **vegetarian by default** by 2025

---

## 8. Waste and Recycling Management

- Segregated glass collection increased to **284 tonnes/year**
- Food waste diverted to **anaerobic digestion** to produce electricity
- Waste diverted from landfill increased through **Warp-It reuse network** (CO₂ savings: 170,000 kg)

---

## 9. Active Travel and Commuting

- Supported through **Move University** and **Active Travel Action Plan**
- Ongoing assessment of:
  - Bike infrastructure
  - Electric bike trials
  - Staff/student commuting emissions

---

## 10. Climate Education & Engagement

- Launched **Sustainability Champions Network** (125+ staff)
- **Sustainability Fortnight** included panels, workshops, and careers events
- **Staff Climate Literacy Training** and induction module rolling out in 2025

---

## 11. Student-Led Sustainability

- **Sustainable Halls Campaign** reached 494 students in 2023–24
- **ICU Sustainability Strategy 2024–28** launched to empower students
- Events:
  - Green Careers Fair (450+ attendees)
  - Climate Youth Summit
  - Clothing swap campaigns and fast-fashion awareness

---

## 12. Sustainability in Research & Partnerships

- Founded multiple research centres:
  - **Bezos Centre for Sustainable Protein**
  - **Hitachi Centre for Decarbonisation**
  - **Rio Tinto Centre for Future Materials**
- Zero Pollution Pioneer Awards and Laidlaw Scholars Programme launched
- Developed the **University Zero Index** to evaluate the ethics of energy industry collaborations
